\title{The Science Genome: A Framework for Semantic Scientific Analysis}

\author{Louis W.H. George}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This paper presents a framework for analyzing how scientific ideas are inherited, transformed, and recombined across publication lineages. Publications are represented in a shared semantic space and decomposed into inherited content from cited predecessors plus residual contribution not explained by those predecessors. The framework combines constrained inheritance inference on a citation graph, enabling interpretable edge-level and node-level analysis of knowledge flow. The result is specification for content-aware scientometric analysis that supports lineage tracing, novelty assessment, and reproducible research interpretation.
\end{abstract}
